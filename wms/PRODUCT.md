# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and administrators of fulfilment centres, warehouse managers, pickers using TSD devices, and fulfilment clients in their own cabinet.

## Product Purpose

LOGOFF WMS brings receiving, address storage, stock, FBS/FBO, KIZ, billing, branch management and warehouse operations into one WMS contour. The public page helps a fulfilment-centre owner understand what will become controllable after implementation and start a conversation.

## Positioning

The system is deployed on the customer's VPS and adapted to the actual warehouse workflow without breaking its logistical chain. It is built from the daily operating practice of LOGOFF's own fulfilment centre.

## Operating Context

The product is used in warehouses and offices across multiple branches. Work includes marketplace orders for Wildberries, Ozon and Yandex, stock and box control, KIZ scanning, pallet-sort and address storage, warehouse transfer, TSD workflows, label printing, client reporting and invoices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include branches with access rules, separate stock visibility, FBS/FBO/DBS workflows, TSD support, barcode and KIZ operations, billing, client cabinet, exports, reports, integrations and configurable themes. The public landing must preserve a working login action and links to the applications. The operational WMS must remain performant and separate from marketing-only visual effects.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is LOGOFF WMS. The audience values individual implementation, transparent ownership of its server and data, fast but correct module development, and support during launch. ## Evidence on Hand

The codebase contains functional WMS views, synthetic interface demonstrations for the public page and screenshots supplied in prior conversations. No customer testimonials, benchmark claims or published pricing are available for the landing.

## Product Principles

- Show the real path of goods, orders and responsibility rather than abstract dashboard claims.
- Preserve a warehouse's operating logic while making it visible and controllable.
- Keep infrastructure and data under the customer's control.
- Build useful modules quickly without replacing reliable operations with shortcuts.
