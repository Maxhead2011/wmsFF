# Card artwork

Print-ready playing cards by Adrian Kennard (RevK), based on nineteenth-century Goodall & Son designs.

Source: https://www.me.uk/cards/
Downloaded standard SVG deck: https://www.me.uk/cards/makeadeck.cgi?zip=
License: CC0 / public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Retrieved 2026-09-08. Only the 36 Durak faces and one card back are distributed.
The source files are retained unchanged. PNG copies are rasterizations for Unreal's UI.
